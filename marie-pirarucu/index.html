THE SPACE BETWEEN ADVENTURES

Remote.
Thoughtfully connected.

Begin with a boutique hotel night in Manaus before flying to Rio Marié. A charter connection to Tefé and onward transfer are planned to bring you to Pirarucú on the same day, without an overnight stop in Manaus.

Final connections depend on flight availability and your confirmed itinerary. After Pirarucú, return from Tefé to Manaus by commercial flight.

BEFORE YOUR FIRST CAST

A few things
you may be wondering.

Do I have to book both destinations?+

No. Rio Marié and Pirarucú can also be booked independently. The combined journey lets you experience both fisheries on the same trip.

Can I choose a shorter Pirarucú extension?+

Yes. Choose 4 nights with 3 full fishing days or 7 nights with 6 full fishing days, subject to availability on your departure.

Are the conservation fees included?+

Yes. The displayed totals include the conservation fees. All rates are per angler in USD, based on double occupancy. Request the complete itinerary for all package inclusions.

Am I committing to a booking by enquiring?+

No. The form is an enquiry for itinerary, rate and travel information. You can discuss the departure and program options with our team before deciding.
